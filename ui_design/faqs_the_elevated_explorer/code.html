Expedition Logistics

What defines a "Curated Expedition"? expand_more

Our expeditions go beyond standard sightseeing. Each journey is limited to 8 guests and led by a subject matter expert—ranging from conservationists to historians. We prioritize deep immersion over rapid transit, ensuring you spend significant time in each location rather than rushing between markers.

Are international flights included? expand_more

To allow our guests the flexibility of using miles or extending their travel independently, international airfare is not included. However, all regional transfers, private charters, and internal flights mentioned in the itinerary are fully managed and included in your booking.

Flexibility & Policy

What is your cancellation grace period? expand_more

We offer a 48-hour "Change of Heart" grace period after booking where your deposit is 100% refundable. Beyond that, cancellations made up to 90 days before departure are eligible for a 75% credit toward a future expedition.

Do you require travel insurance? expand_more

Yes. For the safety of all guests and to protect your investment, comprehensive travel insurance—including medical evacuation coverage—is mandatory for all Elevated Explorer journeys. We can provide recommendations for premium providers upon request.

Preparation is part of the journey.

Our field guides provide a bespoke packing list for your specific ecosystem 60 days before you depart.

The Explorer's Wardrobe

Is there a dress code for dinners? expand_more

We embrace "Safari Chic"—elegant yet practical. While formal attire is never required, we recommend lightweight linens and smart-casual wear for evening meals at our partner lodges and camps.

What specialized gear should I bring? expand_more

We provide high-end binoculars and field equipment. We recommend guests bring well-broken-in waterproof trekking boots and a quality camera. A detailed, destination-specific gear guide will be sent to you upon registration.

Our Ethical Compass

How do you support local communities? expand_more

15% of every booking fee goes directly to local land conservancies or heritage preservation funds. We exclusively employ local guides and partner with locally-owned lodges to ensure the economic benefits of tourism remain within the community.

What is your carbon offset strategy? expand_more

All regional travel within our expeditions is carbon-neutral. We invest in verified reforestation projects in the regions we visit, effectively sequestering 120% of the carbon generated during our ground and regional air transportation.
