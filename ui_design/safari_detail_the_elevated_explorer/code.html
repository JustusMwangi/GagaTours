Serengeti Soul: A Curator's Journey

An immersive expedition through the heart of Tanzania, designed for the discerning traveler who seeks silence as much as spectacle.

The Itinerary

01

Arrival in Arusha

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, your private concierge will escort you to the legendary Legendary Lodge. Settle into your colonial-style garden cottage nestled within a lush coffee plantation.

restaurant Dinner hotel Legendary Lodge
02

The Crater's Edge

Fly by private charter to the Ngorongoro Crater. This "Eden of Africa" hosts over 30,000 animals. Your home is the architectural marvel, Ngorongoro Crater Lodge, where stilted suites offer ethereal views of the mist-covered floor.

restaurant Full Board hotel &Beyond Crater Lodge
05

The Endless Plains

Deep immersion into the Serengeti. Follow the Great Migration herds as they traverse the Grumeti River. Experience walking safaris led by Maasai trackers, discovering the intricate details of the bush often missed from a vehicle.
