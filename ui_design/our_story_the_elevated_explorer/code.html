The Curators

Founded by Julian and Elena Thorne, The Elevated Explorer was born from a decade of deep immersion in the world's most remote landscapes. They sought to bridge the gap between uncompromising comfort and raw, authentic adventure.

With backgrounds in conservation biology and luxury hospitality, they curate experiences that honor the earth while pampering the soul.

Ethical Guiding
