Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you book a tour, create an account, or subscribe to our journal. This may include:

  • check_circle Contact details (Name, email, physical address)
  • check_circle Travel preferences and past expedition history
  • check_circle Passport and identification for international travel arrangements

2. The Concierge Experience

Your data is never sold. We use your information exclusively to curate your personal travel experience. This includes coordinating with local lodges, securing private transit, and tailoring our editorial Journal content to your interests.

Our Promise

Your privacy is the ultimate luxury. We handle your details with the utmost discretion.

3. Cookie & Tracking Policy

We use minimal, functional cookies to remember your preferences and analyze how visitors interact with our Journal. These insights allow us to improve the digital curation of our tours without compromising your identity.

Booking & Expeditions

Deposit and Payments

A 30% non-refundable deposit is required at the time of booking to secure exclusive lodge availability and private guides. Final payment is due 90 days prior to departure.

Cancellation Policy

Expeditions cancelled within 60 days of departure are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. Cancellations within 30 days are non-refundable due to pre-committed logistical costs.

Traveler Responsibility

By booking with The Elevated Explorer, you acknowledge that travel to remote destinations involves inherent risks. Travel insurance is mandatory for all guests.

Intellectual Property

All content, photography, and curated itineraries featured on this platform are the exclusive property of The Elevated Explorer. Reproduction or unauthorized use of our signature travel designs is strictly prohibited.

Questions regarding our terms?

Our legal concierge is available to clarify any aspect of our privacy standards or booking conditions.
